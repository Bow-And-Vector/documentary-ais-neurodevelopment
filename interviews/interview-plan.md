# Interview plan

## Priority interview targets

- endocrinologist or geneticist with expertise in disorders/differences of sex development
- neuroscientist studying prenatal hormone effects on brain development
- person with partial or mild AIS speaking about lived experience
- parent or guardian of a child with AIS or a related developmental condition
- policy expert on Texas gender-affirming care restrictions
- pediatrician or clinician familiar with treatment questions in the state policy context

## Preparation checklist

- confirm topic areas and biography
- prepare a neutral, respectful interview protocol
- allow time for personal story and scientific explanation
- record consent and use agreement
- flag any quotes requiring context or verification
