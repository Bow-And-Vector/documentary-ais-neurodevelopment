- "The science shows this. Prenatal androgens shape not just the body but the brain. And they shape it in ways that aren't always binary."
- "What happens when the government passes laws based on a false understanding of biology? Real people get hurt. Real families get torn apart. Real doctors can't do their jobs."
- "On November 5th, Texas votes for a governor who will either continue these restrictions or reject them."
- "Before early voting starts on October 15th, I want Texas voters to understand: this isn't about ideology. This is about biology. This is about being honest about what the science actually shows."
- "If you're voting in Texas, please think about this. Please vote for science. Please vote for people like me."
