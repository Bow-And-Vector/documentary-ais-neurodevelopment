# Documentary treatment

## Working concept

A documentary that begins with the developmental science of sex differentiation and ends with a close look at how public policy in Texas and nationally is being shaped by simplistic assumptions about sex and gender.

## Narrative arc

1. The basic biology of sex differentiation
2. The science of androgen signaling and receptor variation
3. What AIS reveals about the limits of binary thinking
4. The developing brain and the possibility of mixed or atypical neural organization
5. The lived experience of people with sexual development differences
6. The policy consequences of medical misinformation and legal restriction
7. What a more scientifically literate public conversation might look like

## Tone

- respectful
- rigorous
- emotionally grounded
- clear and accessible
- not polemical for its own sake
