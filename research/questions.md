# Research questions

Use this document to track the central questions your documentary needs to answer.

## Core scientific questions

- What is androgen insensitivity syndrome, and how does it occur?
- How does prenatal androgen exposure affect human development?
- What does current evidence say about neural differences linked to sex hormone exposure?
- How should scientists discuss complexity without oversimplifying the male/female binary?

## Core policy questions

- What legal and political arguments are being made in Texas about gender-diverse care for minors?
- What does the science say about care decisions for trans and gender-diverse youth?
- How are medical organizations responding to policies that restrict treatment?
- What role do misinterpretations of sex and gender play in current legislation?

## Core human story questions

- What does it mean to live with a body and a brain that do not map neatly onto one binary model?
- How do affected people describe their experience of sex development and identity?
- What does family experience look like when a child has developmental variation that is poorly understood by the public?

## Research tasks

- Build a bibliography of key medical papers and reviews.
- Track policy documents and legislative changes.
- Identify interview candidates with relevant expertise.
- Separate high-confidence science from contested interpretation.
