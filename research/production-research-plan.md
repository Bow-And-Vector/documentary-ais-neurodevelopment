# Production research plan

## Research objectives

1. Establish a fact-based understanding of AIS, androgen receptor function, and prenatal sexual differentiation.
2. Identify the strongest available sources on prenatal hormone effects on the brain.
3. Understand the Texas policy environment and how it frames gender-diverse youth care.
4. Map the documentary’s central narrative claims to source material.
5. Build a practical interview list and source index for script development.

## Research pillars

### Pillar 1: Biological and developmental science
- sex differentiation in human development
- androgen receptor function
- disorders/differences of sex development (DSD) and AIS
- the spectrum of AIS phenotypes
- the limits and strengths of prenatal hormone research

### Pillar 2: Human experience and embodiment
- narratives from people with AIS, DSD, or related variation
- family and medical experiences
- the cost of social misreading and medical uncertainty

### Pillar 3: Medical and political policy context
- Texas law and state policy on gender-diverse care for minors
- broader national policy debate
- major medical association guidance and public health positions
- impact on children, families, and clinicians

## Research workflow

1. Build a source list with categories and confidence levels.
2. Identify the strongest 10–20 sources for the science section.
3. Identify 5–10 sources for the Texas policy section.
4. Extract the key fact claims for script use.
5. Match each claim to a source and note where it appears in the documentary arc.
6. Use the source tracker to monitor factual accuracy as the script develops.

## Priority source sets

### Scientific / medical
- endocrinology review articles
- AIS clinical literature and case reports
- androgen receptor and gonadal differentiation references
- developmental neuroscience reviews
- peer-reviewed studies on prenatal hormonal effects

### Policy / medical law
- Texas legislative text and guidance
- legal commentary and court rulings
- association statements on youth gender-affirming care
- health policy analysis and public statements

### Human stories
- interviews, patient accounts, support groups, and family testimony

## Script fact mapping

Each script section should map to at least one verified source category.

Example:

- AIS overview → clinical review + endocrine guidance
- androgen receptor function → receptor biology/review article
- fetal hormone exposure and brain development → neuroscience review
- Texas policy restriction → legislative text + reporting + legal analysis
- lived experience → interview or personal narrative source

## Research deliverables

- fact-checked research brief
- script-ready source list
- interview target list
- policy timeline and key legal references
- evidence matrix for major claims
